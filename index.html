Hi, I'm Mikaela Durana!

I’m a second-year Computer Engineering student and a beginner programmer with a growing love for travel and storytelling.

I started Miles Away as a personal blog project for one of my courses—something I originally built as a requirement, but now plan to continue and expand as I explore more places and experiences.

I may not have traveled internationally much yet, but it’s one of my biggest goals in life to see the world, one place at a time.

I’m currently based in Quezon Province, Philippines, and most of my stories begin right here at home, with my family.

All of the writing and photos on this site come from my own personal experiences—every memory, every lesson, and every mile traveled.

I also keep a growing bucket list of destinations I hope to visit someday… and slowly, I’m working my way through it.
